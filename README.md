# Chinese Invention Patent Drafter

A Skill for drafting and revising Chinese invention patent application materials.

This skill helps produce structured Chinese patent drafting outputs, including invention disclosure summaries, claim sets, descriptions, abstracts, prior-art comparison tables, claim-positioning strategies, examination-risk notes, and attorney-review-ready draft text.

## What it does

- Drafts Chinese invention patent claims, descriptions, abstracts, and full application documents
- Generates novelty-aware claim strategies
- Produces prior-art search query plans in Chinese and English
- Separates user-provided facts, research-confirmed facts, and drafting assumptions
- Adds examination-risk notes and quality-control checklists
- Avoids copying reference patent wording or unsupported experimental claims

## Best for

- Chinese invention patent drafting
- CNIPA-style application preparation
- Claim strategy brainstorming
- Prior-art-aware technical positioning
- Patent draft revision and anti-plagiarism rewriting

## Not legal advice

This skill is a drafting assistant only. It does not provide legal advice, guarantee patent grant, guarantee freedom to operate, or replace review by a qualified Chinese patent attorney or patent agent.
